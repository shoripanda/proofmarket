## The verdict reaches the agent's user (PR #15, 01 §4.27)

A worker's submission is reviewed and the answer becomes final, but the agent's user only learns it if they ask. MCP has no server-to-conversation channel, so:
- `GET /v1/verifications/{id}` carries `summary: { ja, en }`, one paragraph a person can read (who is on it, how many submissions are under AI review or sent back, the final answer and the review verdict, the proof link), and `witness_progress.checking / returned`.
- `GET …?wait=0–45` long-polls; MCP `get_reality_verification` waits up to 45 s; `request_reality_verification` takes `wait_seconds`.
- Tool descriptions and every response's `note` tell the agent to repeat `summary`, `result.reviews` and the proof link to its user as they change, without being asked.

## Incident: tick 500 for three hours (PR #16)

PR #10 bound a JavaScript `Date` into a raw SQL fragment; postgres-js sent it as `toString()` text and Postgres rejected it; the tick failed on its second step every minute from 13:29 UTC and no settlement job ran. Full account in `docs/incident-2026-10-08-tick-500.md`. Fix: ISO text in the fragment, every tick step isolated, the error's cause chain logged.
